FAQ

Questions people ask

How does verification work?

When someone reports a found item, they keep a few details private (like what's inside a wallet) and write questions about them. To claim the item you answer those questions and can upload proof such as a bill or an old photo of you with the item. The finder or an admin compares your answers with the hidden details before approving.

Where do I collect my item?

Always at the {{ security_desk }} ({{ security_hours }}). Once your claim is approved you get a 6-digit handover code by email. Bring your college ID card, tell the code to the security staff, and they'll mark the item as returned.

What happens with false claims?

Every claim is tied to a verified college email. Each rejected claim lowers your trust score, and after {{ max_rejected }} rejected claims your account can no longer submit claims. Admins can also ban accounts that misuse the system.

Who can use this?

Anyone with a {{ college_name }} email address{% if allowed_domains %} (ending in {% for d in allowed_domains %}@{{ d }}{% if not loop.last %}, {% endif %}{% endfor %}){% endif %}. You verify your email with a one-time code when you register.

What if two people claim the same item?

The claim is marked as disputed and only an admin can decide. They compare both sets of answers and proof, and may ask both people to visit the security desk.
